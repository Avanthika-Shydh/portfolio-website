Retail Analytics for Optimizing Staffing and Sales

Conducted a comprehensive retail analytics study for a physical clothing brand, analyzing real-world data points across footfall patterns, seasonal sales, and local competitor pricing. Utilized Python (Pandas/NumPy) for exploratory data analysis, revealing critical operational inefficiencies like peak-hour staffing gaps. Delivered actionable, data-backed strategies that optimized staffing schedules and improved overall profit margins.

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Scikit-learn
  • Excel
  • Business Analytics & Insights
